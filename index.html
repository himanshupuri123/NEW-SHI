About The Event

What is Smart India Hackathon?

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE to provide students a platform to solve pressing real-world challenges.

🚩

Government of India Initiative

World's Biggest Open Innovation Model

SIH is designed to inculcate a culture of product innovation and a mindset of problem-solving. It allows students to work on challenges presented by Ministries and leading tech multinationals.

🏛️

The ICFAI University, Jaipur

HOST & ORGANIZER

Providing state-of-the-art labs, high-speed internet, and mentorship to empower students during the 16-hour internal university implementation round.

OFFICIAL GUIDELINES

Announcement

Please carefully read all the rules and eligibility criteria before registering your team.

1

The last date for registration is September 15th. After the last date, students will not be allowed to participate. Teams must include at least one girl candidate.

2

Students can only register through the official website, not through any other means or offline forms.

3

The inter-college/inner-college hackathon updates and schedules will be announced soon on the portal.

4

Students must arrive in proper college attire along with their required personal electronic devices.

5

In case of any emergency, students should immediately contact the coordinating teachers or student team leads.

6

Important update for the internal hackathon: There is a mandatory 16-hour implementation round within the university where student teams must fully implement their products.

HACKATHON DOMAINS

PROBLEM STATEMENTS AND PRIZES TO BE WON

Explore problem statements and winning rewards.

1

SIH 2026 brings you problem statements from leading public and private organizations, union ministries, state governments, and NGOs in India. Teams must include at least one girl candidate.

2

There would be ONE single winning team for every problem statement posted on the portal. The prize money will be given by the collaborating ministry/industry ONLY IF that organization approves and likes the winning team's idea.

3

Attractive prize amounts will be awarded corresponding to each winning problem statement category.

4

Teams are encouraged to think out-of-the-box and provide creative solutions under the ‘Students Innovation’ category. Note that a maximum of 100 teams are allowed from a single college.

5

Prize money for the student innovation category will be Rs. 100,000/- per theme.

6

Participating teams must successfully clear the internal university 16-hour product implementation stage to qualify for further rounds.

OFFICIAL GUIDELINES

Rules & Regulation

Please carefully read all the rules and eligibility criteria before registering your team.

1

All team members should be from the same college; no inter-college teams are allowed. Cross-branch members are encouraged with at least one girl candidate.

2

Each team must mandatorily comprise 6 members, including the team leader and mandatory female participation.

3

For the Software Edition, after teams are shortlisted, each team will have the option of selecting 2 mentors from industry or academia.

4

If a team is selected for the final Hackathon, members will need to travel to the assigned nodal center anywhere across India.

5

Shortlisted teams traveling for the grand finale will be eligible to receive reimbursement for travel via 2nd Class sleeper railway fare.

6

Reimbursement will be paid at the grand finale nodal centers upon producing original train tickets and completing necessary claim forms.
